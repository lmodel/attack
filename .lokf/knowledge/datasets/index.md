# Datasets

* [attack LinkML schema](attack-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML schema of MITRE ATT&CK v20.0 in STIX 2.1, `src/attack/schema/attack.yaml` (schema id `https://w3id.org/lmodel/attack`): 24 classes with the Attack prefix, each object class extending a lmodel/stix class, 12 enums and 37 slots, most of them ATT&CK's `x_mitre_*` keys. Its `lokf adapt` copy `.lokf/attack.yaml`, rooted on StixObject and StixBundle, is the bundle's domain schema.
* [lmodel/stix modules](stix-modules.md) - The six modules of lmodel/stix's STIX 2.1 schema that the ATT&CK schema imports, copied unchanged into `src/attack/schema/stix/` from stix commit 267064d and held to their git object hashes by `tests/test_stix_import.py`.
* [Pinned ATT&CK v20.0 bundles](pinned-attack-bundles.md) - The three STIX bundles of ATT&CK v20.0 (Enterprise, Mobile and ICS), kept out of git and pinned by address, size, SHA-256 and git object hash in `tests/data/attack-stix-data/manifest.json`; `just fetch-attack-data` downloads them into `tmp/attack-stix-data/`.
* [Test data and tests](test-data.md) - The tests and their data under `tests/`: 21 valid examples and 72 counter-examples, an extract of 97 objects of the v20.0 bundles, the checks of the ATT&CK Data Model's rules beyond the validator, the lists of where the data departs from it, and the neighbours' adapt copies.
* [Generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - What `just gen-project` and `just gen-doc` write from the schema: the formats under `project/`, the Python dataclasses and Pydantic models under `src/attack/datamodel/`, `docs/schema/attack.yaml` and the schema pages under `docs/elements/`; never edited by hand.
